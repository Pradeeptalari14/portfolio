# Studio #349: Pigouvian Automation Tax & The AI Layoff Trap

Interactive Macroeconomic & DevSecOps Simulation Engine modeling the Corporate Prisoner's Dilemma, aggregate demand destruction, and optimal Pigouvian taxation under autonomous AI displacement.

## Academic Reference
Based directly on research by:
* **Brett Hemenway Falk** (University of Pennsylvania)
* **Gerry Tsoukalas** (Boston University Questrom School of Business / Wharton)
* Paper: *"The AI Layoff Trap"* (2026)

## Key Formulations
* Microeconomic Margin Arbitrage: $\Delta \text{Margin} = w_h - c_{AI}$
* Macroeconomic Speed Governor: $\tau^* = \text{MPC} \times (w_h - c_{AI}) \times (1 - 1/N)$
* 100% Tax Recycling into human-in-the-loop retraining and transition dividends.
