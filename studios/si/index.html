What is Synthetic Intelligence (SI)? In this catalogue, Synthetic Intelligence refers to engineered non-biological cognition developed from first principles — combining neuro-symbolic reasoning, Global Workspace Theory cognitive architectures, active inference world models, and emergent collective intelligence.

Not to be confused with: "SI" is sometimes informally used in online discussions as an abbreviation for Superintelligence. In this learning hub, hypothetical superintelligence research is catalogued strictly under ASI (Artificial Superintelligence) to prevent conceptual confusion.

Note on Taxonomy: Synthetic Intelligence is an active frontier research discipline and is not a universally standardized industry category; it bridges cognitive science, formal mathematical logic, and artificial neural representation.
