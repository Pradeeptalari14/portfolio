🎯 1. The Forward Deployed Engineering Imperative

In Fortune 500 enterprises, 85% of GenAI prototypes stall before reaching production. The bottleneck is rarely the model weights—it is enterprise compliance, air-gapped VPC networking, lack of PII redaction, ungrounded hallucinations, and lack of stakeholder alignment. A Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE) embeds directly within client organizations to eliminate this friction through a rigorous 5-phase delivery model.

⚙️ 2. Air-Gapped Architecture & Zero-Egress Network Isolation

Regulated industries (Healthcare, Banking, GovCloud) forbid streaming sensitive data to public cloud APIs. This system packages the entire AI runtime—FastAPI gateway, local vLLM inference engine, Qdrant vector database, and Presidio PII scrubbers—inside private Kubernetes clusters with zero outbound internet egress.

🛡️ 3. Continuous Evaluation & Automated Acceptance Gates
